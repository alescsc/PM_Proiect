G1=M/IO, G2A=G2B=GND

(Y1..Y6 neconectate)

SRAM_SEL = Y0 OR A16
 Y0 A16 | SRAM_SEL
 0  0   | 0 (00000H-0FFFFH)
 0  1   | 1 (10000H-1FFFFH, nimic)
 1  x   | 1

Banca (A0 si BHE):
 BHE A0 | acces
 0   0  | cuvant 16 biti (2 banci)
 0   1  | octet impar (D8-D15)
 1   0  | octet par (D0-D7)
 1   1  | niciunul

Selectii (active pe 0):
 CS_SRAM_P  = SRAM_SEL OR A0
 CS_SRAM_I  = SRAM_SEL OR BHE
 CS_EPROM_P = Y7 OR A0
 CS_EPROM_I = Y7 OR BHE

Porti OR 74LS32:

      Y7+A0 -> CS_EPROM_P
